# User Personas

User personas represent the main types of people who will use the BLBC Booking System. They help ensure the system is designed to meet the needs of its intended users.

## Persona 1 – Visitor

**Name:** Sarah Johnson

**Role:** Member of the Public

**Goals:**

* Find a suitable room for hire.
* Check room availability.
* Submit a booking request quickly and easily.

**Frustrations:**

* Unclear room availability.
* Complicated booking processes.

---

## Persona 2 – Church Administrator

**Name:** David Smith

**Role:** Church Administrator

**Goals:**

* Review booking requests.
* Approve or reject bookings.
* Manage room information and availability.

**Frustrations:**

* Time spent managing bookings manually.
* Double bookings.
* Responding to repeated booking enquiries.

---

## Persona 3 – Church Leadership

**Role:** Church Leadership Team

**Goals:**

* Increase the use of church facilities.
* Generate additional income through room hire.
* Reduce the administrative workload.

**Frustrations:**

* Underused rooms.
* Inefficient booking processes.
* Limited visibility of room usage.
